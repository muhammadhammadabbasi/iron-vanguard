IRON VANGUARD  v0.2  -  Tactical stealth shooter
=================================================

Missions in this build (1-3 of a 20-mission campaign):
  01 BLACKOUT      - HALO jump from a C-130, blow the radio mast, steal the laptop, helicopter extraction
  02 SILENT PASS   - Black Hawk insertion, mountain village, checkpoint, keycard, meet Lt. Rauf
  03 THE WATCHERS  - Hilltop radar station at dusk: shut down cameras, C4 the radar, extract

CONTROLS
  Move W A S D | Look Mouse | Fire / Aim Left / Right mouse | Reload R
  Weapons 1-5 or mouse wheel (1 Knife, 2 M9 silenced, 3 M4A1, 4 AKM, 5 SVD sniper)
  Sprint Shift | Crouch C | Jump Space | Grenade G | Medkit H
  Binoculars B (tags enemies on the map) | Satellite map: hold M or Tab
  Interact: hold E | Parachute: Space to open, A/D steer, S brake | Pause Esc | Fullscreen F11

TIPS
  - Guards who spot you run to the alarm (siren pole). Shoot the siren to silence it.
  - Blowing up radio masts/antennas stops enemy reinforcements.
  - Knife an unaware guard from behind for a silent kill. The M9 is silenced.
  - Health does not regenerate - use medkits. Dead enemies drop weapons and ammo.

PERFORMANCE
  Settings > Graphics: LOW for Intel UHD / integrated graphics, MEDIUM/HIGH for a graphics card.
